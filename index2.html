Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
What Payment Methods Are Available?

You can pay online or choose to pay on delivery. Our JumiaPay platform supports cards (Visa, MasterCard, Verve), bank transfers, and USSD payments for maximum convenience.

Can I Return Items I Purchase?

Yes! You can return eligible items within 7 days of purchase for free and receive a full refund. This applies to items from both Jumia Official Stores and other sellers.

When is Jumia Black Friday?

Jumia Black Friday happens every November and offers the biggest discounts across categories like fashion, electronics, and appliances. Enjoy games, treasure hunts, and unbeatable deals throughout the event.

Is Free Delivery Available Nationwide?

Customers in cities like Lagos, Abuja, Ibadan, Warri, Benin, Abeokuta, Akure, and Port Harcourt enjoy free delivery on items with the “Free Delivery” tag. Note: Large items like refrigerators and washing machines are excluded.

How Can I Contact Jumia Customer Service?

Our support team is here for you. For inquiries, call 0201-888-1106. To place an order, call 0700-600-0000. Prefer chatting? Use the Jumia App for instant support.

How Can I Become a Seller on Jumia?

Start selling on Jumia today! Register on our website and follow the simple steps. Reach thousands of customers and grow your business with us.

What Other Major Events Does Jumia Host?

In addition to Black Friday, Jumia Tech Week is a huge annual event held in March, offering discounts on the latest gadgets and electronics. We also celebrate Ramadan, Easter, Christmas, and other seasonal holidays with special offers and promotions.
